Key Differences:

  • Insecure: Passwords stored in plain text - anyone can read them!
  • Secure: Passwords hashed with bcrypt - cannot be reversed
  • Insecure: Credit cards "encrypted" with base64 (just encoding, not encryption)
  • Secure: Credit cards encrypted with AES-256 - requires key to decrypt
  • Insecure: All data visible in database
  • Secure: Sensitive data properly protected
